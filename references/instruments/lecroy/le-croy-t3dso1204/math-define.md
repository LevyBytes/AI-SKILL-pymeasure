# pymeasure.instruments.lecroy.LeCroyT3DSO1204.math_define

[Owning PyMeasure class, adapter and connection contract](overview.md)

*property *math_define*: Any*

Choose the waveform math operation applied between two channels.

Pass three parameters together as a tuple:

1. source1 : left-side source channel

2. operation : operator must be “*”, “/”, “+”, “-”

3. source2 : right-side source channel
