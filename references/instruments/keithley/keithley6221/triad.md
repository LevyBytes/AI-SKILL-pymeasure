# pymeasure.instruments.keithley.Keithley6221.triad

[Owning PyMeasure class, adapter and connection contract](overview.md)

triad(*base_frequency*, *duration*)

Play a musical triad through the system beep.

Parameters:

- **base_frequency** – Frequency in Hz, constrained between 65 Hz and 1.3 MHz

- **duration** – Time in seconds, constrained between 0 and 7.9 seconds
