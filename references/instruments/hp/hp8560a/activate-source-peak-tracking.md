# pymeasure.instruments.hp.HP8560A.activate_source_peak_tracking

[Owning PyMeasure class, adapter and connection contract](overview.md)

activate_source_peak_tracking()

Run a routine that automatically adjusts both coarse-tracking and fine-tracking controls to maximize the tracking generator response on the spectrum-analyzer display. Tracking peak is unnecessary for resolution bandwidths greater than or equal to 300 kHz. Make a thru connection before peaking to preserve accuracy.

Note

Available only with an HP 8560A Option 002.
