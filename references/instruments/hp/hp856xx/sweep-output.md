# pymeasure.instruments.hp.hp856Xx.HP856Xx.sweep_output

HP856Xx.sweep_output

Select the sweep-related signal output available at rear-panel J8. FAV outputs a dc ramp of 0.5V/GHz. RAMP outputs a 0—10 V ramp that corresponds to the sweep ramp tuning the first local oscillator (LO). On the HP 8561B, multiband sweeps provide one ramp per frequency band.

Type: `str` or `SweepOut`
