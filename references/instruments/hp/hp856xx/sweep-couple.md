# pymeasure.instruments.hp.hp856Xx.HP856Xx.sweep_couple

HP856Xx.sweep_couple

Control sweep couple mode. Choose stimulus-response operation or the spectrum-analyzer mode that uses auto-coupled sweep time. In stimulus-response mode, auto-coupled times are usually faster for swept-response measurements. These stimulus-response auto-coupled sweep times are typically valid when the measurement frequency span is less than 20 times the device-under-test bandwidth.

Type: `str` or `SweepCoupleMode`
