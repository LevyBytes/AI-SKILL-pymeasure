# pymeasure.instruments.hp.HP8561B.mixer_bias

[Owning PyMeasure class, adapter and connection contract](overview.md)

*property *mixer_bias*: Any*

Set external-mixer bias when efficient mixer operation requires diode bias. MBIAS activates the bias on the center conductor of the IF input. A `+` or `-` appears at the left edge of the spectrum analyzer display to show positive or negative bias. Turning bias off sets MBIAS to 0. Default units are milliamps.
