## Related Projects

### Related Projects

PyMeasure belongs to a wider group of laboratory automation tools:

-

[LECO Protocol](https://github.com/pymeasure/leco-protocol): Open standard for laboratory equipment communication, built around modern protocols

-

[pyleco](https://github.com/pymeasure/pyleco): Python library that implements LECO for distributed instrument control systems

-

[Python Lab Automation Landscape](https://github.com/pymeasure/python-lab-automation-landscape): Curated collection covering Python tools for laboratory automation and instrumentation

For robust laboratory automation systems, these projects add capabilities that complement PyMeasure.

## Authors

### Authors

Colin Jermain and Graham Rowlands began PyMeasure at Cornell University in 2013 after realizing they were separately developing similar Python packages for scientific measurements. PyMeasure unified that work and has continued to receive useful contributions from scientists interested in advancing measurement software.
